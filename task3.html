Norman Foster

1935 - present

Norman Foster is a British architect known for his modern and high-tech buildings. He founded Foster + Partners and designed projects in many countries. His work combines technology, practical design, and respect for the surrounding city. Foster has made an important contribution to contemporary architecture.

Important dates

  1. 1935 - Norman Foster was born in Manchester, England.
  2. 1961 - He completed his studies at Yale University.
  3. 1967 - Foster Associates was founded.
  4. 1990 - He received a knighthood for his contribution to architecture.
  5. 1999 - He was awarded the Pritzker Architecture Prize.

Main achievements

  • Designed the Khan Shatyr is a stylish tent-shaped entertainment center in Astana.
  • Created the modern Reichstag dome in Berlin.
  • Designed the Millau Viaduct in France.
  • Developed sustainable and energy-efficient architectural solutions.
